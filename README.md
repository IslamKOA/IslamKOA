# Hi, I'm Islam KOA 👋

**PhD researcher | Remote sensing | Photogrammetry | Geodesy | Open-source geospatial software**

📍 Grenoble, France · 🏛️ INRAE / Institut des Géosciences de l’Environnement (IGE), Université Grenoble Alpes

I develop reproducible geospatial workflows for satellite imagery, digital elevation models (DEMs), LiDAR, and geodetic reference data. My research addresses high-relief mountain terrain, geomorphic change, erosion and deposition, and sediment budgets.

## 🛰️ Featured open-source projects

### [WASA-DEM — Satellite Stereo DSM Workflow](https://github.com/IslamKOA/pleiades-asp-workflow)
Reproducible optical satellite stereo and tri-stereo photogrammetry, point-cloud reconstruction, and DSM generation with NASA Ames Stereo Pipeline (ASP). Supports Pléiades, Pléiades NEO and SPOT 6/7.

[Code and installation](https://github.com/IslamKOA/pleiades-asp-workflow) · [Research software DOI](https://doi.org/10.57745/4OQDHQ)

### [ElevDatum — Geodetic & Elevation Data Toolkit](https://github.com/IslamKOA/geodetic-elevation-data-toolkit)
Geoid and vertical datum transformations, DEM acquisition, elevation processing, survey coordinates and GPS time conversion.

[Code and installation](https://github.com/IslamKOA/geodetic-elevation-data-toolkit) · [Research software DOI](https://doi.org/10.57745/1O0YRE)
